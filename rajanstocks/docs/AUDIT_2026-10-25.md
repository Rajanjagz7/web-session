## E. NSE/BSE Coverage Report  (priority investigation)

**Data source (verified in code):** `upstox_service.py::_load_equity_keys()` downloads Upstox's public NSE instrument master (`assets.upstox.com/market-quote/instruments/exchange/NSE.json.gz`) once per instance and derives the universe from `segment == "NSE_FO"` option contracts (`underlying_type == INDEX` → indices, else stocks named in `NSE_EQ`). `market_api.ensure_universe()` ingests it every 6 h. The Trade/search/Picks/auto-trader universe is exactly this set.

**Expected universe (from the same master file, downloaded independently during this audit):**

| Set | Master says | App has | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| NSE F&O stock underlyings | 213 | **213** (`/api/m/universe`) | ✅ exact 1:1, zero missing/extra |
| F&O underlyings silently dropped by the name-match (`elif u in names`) | 0 | 0 | ✅ none |
| NSE F&O index underlyings | 6 (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY, NIFTYNXT50, **NIFTYFPI**) | 5 | ❌ **NIFTYFPI missing** → **FIXED** |
| BSE F&O underlyings | 4 (SENSEX, BANKEX, SENSEX50, FOCIT — Upstox BSE file has **0 BSE_EQ equities**; BSE stock options do not exist there) | 1 (SENSEX) | ❌ **BANKEX/SENSEX50/FOCIT missing** → **FIXED** |
| NSE non-F&O equities (~2,504 of 2,717 NSE_EQ) | not derivatives-eligible | excluded | ✅ by design (options app); search/charts follow the same universe |
| BSE equities | 0 with options on Upstox | n/a | provider limitation, not an app defect |

**Root cause of the gaps:** `market_api.INDICES` and `upstox_service.INDEX_KEYS` are hardcoded lists that had drifted from the instrument master (NIFTYFPI options launched; BSE indices never added). Stocks could not drift because they are derived dynamically.

**Fix applied (commit "Exchange coverage: NIFTYFPI + BSE BANKEX/SENSEX50/FOCIT"):** added the 4 missing indices to `INDEX_KEYS` + `INDICES`; verified live option chains on Upstox for each — NIFTYFPI 93 strikes/3 expiries, BANKEX 158/3, SENSEX50 92/3, FOCIT 160/3. Regression tests `test_coverage.py` (3 tests) pin the F&O index set so future drift fails CI.

**Conclusion:** the app's stock universe is the *complete, correct* NSE derivatives-eligible set (213/213). There is **no** silent exclusion of F&O stocks. The only genuine defects were the 4 missing options-eligible indices (now fixed). "All NSE/BSE listed equities" is out of scope by design and is explicitly documented here as such.

## G. Final Summary

- **Confirmed and fixed:** Z Trend chart/backend divergence (high-severity correctness), 4 missing F&O indices, Vedic cold-start stranding. All with regression tests (`test_coverage.py`, `test_indicators.py`, `test_rzt_parity.py`).
- **Confirmed, not fixed (need your decision):** remove or keep the `/api/v1/*` family; whether to add UI buttons for the ops endpoints; whether the auto trader should include SENSEX/NIFTYFPI/BSE indices in `INDEX_SYMS` (currently trades the 5 NSE majors only); re-run the offline Z Trend grid backtest with the corrected seeding.
- **Could not be verified:** live in-session behaviour (09:15–15:30 items — sell-legs at 09:20, hedged-spread entries, time-cycle triggers) are marked ☀ in `qa/SMOKE_CHECKLIST_2026-10-24.md` and need a market-hours pass.
- **Dead APIs:** none. Every endpoint has a verified caller (UI, cron, tests/ops).
- **Coverage:** NSE F&O stocks 213/213 exact; indices now 6/6 NSE + 4/4 BSE (Upstox has no BSE stock options; ~2,504 non-F&O NSE equities excluded by design).
- **Performance:** warm endpoints 4–330 ms (heaviest: God's Eye board 330 ms, polled ≤90 s); picks 145 ms; chain 20 ms; oquote cached 4 s and batches ≤30 keys; the only rate-limit pressure point (oquote 503s) degrades gracefully.
- **Remaining risks:** strategy-level textbook validation for all 40+ strategies (primitives + the 4-year accuracy replay cover them indirectly); rzt_backtest JSON stored with pre-fix seeding.
