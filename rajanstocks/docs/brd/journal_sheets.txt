A1: START HERE • PAPER JOURNAL GUIDE
A2: Topic
B2: Instructions
A3: Purpose
B3: A structured journal to validate setups and indicator behaviour in simulation. It does not send orders.
A4: Step 1 — Settings
B4: Set virtual capital, risk per trade, daily loss limit, max entries/day, costs and slippage assumptions.
A5: Step 2 — Trade plan
B5: Before entry, record setup, indicator values, direction, entry, initial SL, targets, TSL method and reason.
A6: Step 3 — Size
B6: Use the risk budget and initial entry-to-SL distance to determine a quantity that fits the cap. Round down to valid lot size.
A7: Step 4 — Manage
B7: Record target hits, partial exits, every TSL move and the actual exit type. Never loosen SL to increase planned risk.
A8: Step 5 — Review
B8: Record net P&L, R multiple, rule adherence, emotion, screenshot/reference and lesson.
A9: TSL models
B9: Fixed SL; breakeven after configured R trigger; step trail; distance trail. Keep same sample when comparing methods.
A10: Ambiguous candles
B10: If a candle touches both stop and target and intrabar sequence is unknown, mark Ambiguous; do not assume the better result.
A11: Risk warning
B11: Default values are examples, not recommendations. Paper fills can differ from live execution due to spread, slippage, gaps and liquidity.
