## Indicators that cannot be replayed honestly

- **Quantum Matrix** - built on 1-minute bars; minute history for past years is not available
- **HA Dancing Trend Channel** - built on 1-minute bars; minute history for past years is not available
- **3M Heikin Ashi Dancing Channel** - built on 1-minute bars; minute history for past years is not available
- **15-Min Opening Range Breakout** - needs the first 15 minutes of each past session (minute bars)
- **VWAP + RSI Mean Reversion** - needs intraday VWAP (minute bars)
- **Open = High / Open = Low** - Open = Low / High is only known during the session, so a daily bar would let the test peek at the future
- **Volume Buzzer (short-term)** - needs 15-minute bars for past years
- **Daily Volume Buzzer** - an alert (no entry / direction), not a trade signal
- **Potential Breakout (200D High)** - an alert (no entry / direction), not a trade signal
- **India VIX Next-Day Range** - an expected-range band, not a buy / sell signal

## Caveats

- Backtests describe the past; they do not predict the future. Paper research, not advice.
- Options results are modelled on the underlying's daily candles (expired option candles are unavailable): spreads, gaps and slippage are not included beyond the stated cost.
- Choosing the best of 8 rules per indicator in-sample then judging out-of-sample reduces, but does not remove, selection luck.
- Win rate alone is not the whole story: with a 1R target a 55% win rate can still lose money after costs; read it together with avg R and profit factor.
